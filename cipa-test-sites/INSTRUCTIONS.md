# CIPA "Use It Like a Customer Would" — Test Sites

This repo hosts 10 pre-built test sites for the CIPA exercise (Usercentrics - WebCMP).

## How to claim a site
1. Open the **Issues** tab in this repo and find "Claim your test site."
2. Comment with your name next to an unclaimed site number (e.g. `site-03 — Jane Doe`).
3. First come, first served — one site per person.

## How to implement your CMP setup
1. Go to your claimed folder, e.g. `site-03/index.html`.
2. Click the pencil (✏️) icon to edit the file directly in GitHub.
3. Paste your CMP script snippet where marked (`<!-- PASTE YOUR CMP SNIPPET HERE -->`) inside the `<head>` section.
4. Click **Commit directly to the main branch**.
5. Wait about a minute, then visit your live site at:
   `https://smeskini123.github.io/cipa-test-sites/site-03/` (swap in your site number)
6. Confirm the CMP banner/setup is working as expected.

No local setup, git knowledge, or coding required — everything happens in the browser.
